### Page 2: Insights

## Key Insights
1. **Standard Class dominates shipping** — handles ~60% of all orders, with First and Second Class making up most of the rest.
2. **Technology and Office Supplies convert sales to profit far better than Furniture** — roughly 14% margin vs. under 7%, a gap that traces back to Furniture's weakest sub-category.
3. **Central leads on both sales and profit by region**; the long tail from Southeast Asia through Canada shows meaningful sales without matching profit — a pricing/discounting gap, not a demand gap.
4. **Profit consistently dips below the sales trend every Aug–Oct** across all four years — a recurring, structural pattern rather than a one-offTables.
5. **Central region leads on profit**; Southeast Asia generates meaningful sales but converts far less of it to profit, pointing to a discounting/pricing-discipline gap rather than a demand problem.
6. **Standard Class shipping** handles ~60% of all orders — the business runs primarily on slower, lower-cost shipping.
